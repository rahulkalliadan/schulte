Train your peripheral vision and improve reading speed by finding numbers in sequence — as fast as you can.

How to play

1 Find and tap numbers 1, 2, 3… in order from the shuffled grid.
2 Timer rounds up to the nearest 10s — 5×5 = 30s, 6×6 = 50s, and so on.
3 Grid grows every 10 levels: 5×5 → 6×6 → 7×7 … up to level 1000.
4 Time runs out → game over. Find all numbers → next level!
