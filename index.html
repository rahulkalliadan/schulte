Schulte
Focus.

Train your peripheral vision and improve reading speed by finding numbers in sequence — as fast as you can.

How to play

1 A grid of shuffled numbers appears. Find and tap them 1, 2, 3… in order.
2 You have a time limit. 5×5 → 25s, 6×6 → 36s, and so on (n² seconds).
3 Levels 1–10 use a 5×5 grid. Every 10 levels the grid grows by one row and column.
4 Run out of time → game over. Complete all numbers → next level!
